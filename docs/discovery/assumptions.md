# Lab 4: Stakeholders, Known Facts, and Assumptions

## Project
Neighborhood Listing Platform
Team E - Echo

## Stakeholder Group 1: Property Professionals
Examples: real estate agents, landlords, and property managers.

### Known Facts
- The course project includes a neighborhood property listing platform.
- The existing application displays fictional sample properties.
- The assignment identifies property professionals as a stakeholder group.

### Assumptions Requiring Confirmation
- Property professionals need an easier way to manage listing information.
- They want to review listings before publication.
- They may need help managing inquiries from prospective renters or buyers.

## Stakeholder Group 2: Local Sponsors
Examples: local businesses and community organizations.

### Known Facts
- The course project includes a sponsor data model.
- The assignment identifies local sponsors as a stakeholder group.

### Assumptions Requiring Confirmation
- Local businesses want to reach people exploring neighborhood properties.
- Sponsors want control over their advertisements and business information.
- Sponsors need a way to review promotional content before publication.

## Discovery and Ethics
- No stakeholder interviews have been completed or verified in this document.
- All unverified needs are working hypotheses, not interview findings.
- Real outreach requires instructor approval and participant consent.
- Simulated interviews must be clearly labeled as simulated.
- Do not collect sensitive personal information or use scraped contacts.

## Questions to Confirm
1. How do property professionals currently manage listings and inquiries?
2. Who approves listing information before publication?
3. How do local sponsors currently promote their businesses?
4. What information must sponsors approve before their content goes live?
5. What accessibility needs should the platform support?
