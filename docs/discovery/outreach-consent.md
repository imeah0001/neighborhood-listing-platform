## Consent Script

Before beginning, explain:

- This is a voluntary academic interview.
- The participant may skip questions or stop.
- Only minimal, de-identified notes will be taken.
- No audio or video will be recorded.
- Notes will be used for academic requirements planning.

Ask: Do you agree to participate?

If consent is not given, do not proceed.

## Restrictions

- Instructor approval is required before outreach.
- No automated calls or bulk emails.
- No scraped contact lists or AI impersonation.
- No sensitive personal or financial information.
- Simulated interviews must be labeled simulated.
