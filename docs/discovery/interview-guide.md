## Final Eight Neutral Interview Questions

1. Property Professional Workflow:
   Can you walk me through how you currently create,
   update, and manage property listings?

2. Local Sponsor Workflow:
   How do you currently organize and share information
   about your business with potential customers?

3. Audience and Key Roles:
   What audience and supporting roles are involved
   in sharing your listing or business information?

4. Content Approval:
   What steps do you follow to check information before
   it is published or shared?

5. Success Measures:
   What indicators help you determine whether your
   current approach is working well?

6. Accessibility:
   How, if at all, do you consider different accessibility
   needs when sharing information?

7. Administrative Bottlenecks:
   Which parts of your current workflow take the most
   time or require repeated effort?

8. Collaboration:
   How do you coordinate changes or requests with
   other people involved in your work?

## Interview Ethics
- Participation must be voluntary.
- Participants may skip questions or withdraw.
- Obtain instructor approval before contacting anyone.
- Do not request client names or sensitive business data.
- Record only minimal, de-identified notes.
- Clearly label simulated interviews as simulated.
