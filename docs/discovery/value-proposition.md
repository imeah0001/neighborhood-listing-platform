# Lab 4: Neighborhood Listing Platform Value Proposition

## Team
Team E - Echo
CIS 3610, Cal State LA

## Project Overview
The Neighborhood Listing Platform is a student-built
prototype that combines property listings with
information about local businesses and sponsors.

## Stakeholder Group 1: Property Professionals
Potential users include real estate agents, landlords,
and property managers.

Proposed value:
- Present property information in a consistent format.
- Help visitors browse and compare listings.
- Support accurate, reviewed listing content.

## Stakeholder Group 2: Local Sponsors
Potential users include local businesses and
community organizations.

Proposed value:
- Display relevant local business information.
- Help neighborhood visitors discover nearby services.
- Support sponsor content review before publication.

## Value Proposition
For property professionals and local sponsors who need
to share trustworthy neighborhood information, the
Neighborhood Listing Platform aims to provide an
accessible, organized directory of property listings
and local business content.

Unlike disconnected listing pages and promotional
materials, the proposed platform brings both types
of information together while emphasizing validation,
accessibility, and content approval.

## Initial Success Measures
Potential measures requiring stakeholder confirmation:
- Accuracy and completeness of published information.
- Ease of finding relevant property and sponsor content.
- Time required to review and update listings.
- Accessibility of essential information.

## Current Evidence and Limitations
- The student prototype already displays fictional listings.
- Property and sponsor data contracts exist in the project.
- The proposed benefits are hypotheses, not verified
  stakeholder interview findings.
- Real stakeholder needs and success measures remain
  subject to discovery and confirmation.
- No real client endorsement or business partnership
  is claimed.
