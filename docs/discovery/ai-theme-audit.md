# Lab 4: AI-Assisted Discovery Theme Audit

## Project
Neighborhood Listing Platform
Team E - Echo

## Evidence Status
SIMULATED PRACTICE MATERIAL - NOT REAL INTERVIEWS

The source notes N1-N8 are hypothetical scenarios created
for academic practice. They are not verified stakeholder
statements. Instructor acceptance of simulated evidence
has not yet been confirmed.

## AI Analysis
Tool: Google AI Studio (Gemini)

Prompt purpose:
Identify themes, uncertainties, and candidate requirements
from eight simulated discovery notes.

## Five Themes Identified by Gemini

1. Multi-channel content maintenance (N1, N5)
2. Content review before publication (N2, N6)
3. Mobile and desktop accessibility (N4)
4. Non-sensitive measures of success (N3, N7)
5. Corrections and change coordination (N3, N8)

## Line-by-Line Source Verification

N1: Property details are updated in multiple places.
AI interpretation: Centralized management may help.
Decision: Plausible inference, not a confirmed need.

N2: Listing review before publication is preferred.
AI interpretation: Draft and review workflow.
Decision: Supported as a simulated preference.

N3: Accuracy and fewer corrections may indicate success.
AI interpretation: Performance and correction tracking.
Decision: The measures are supported; tracking tools
are additional assumptions.

N4: Listings should be readable on mobile and desktop.
AI interpretation: Responsive, accessible interface.
Decision: Responsive viewing is supported. Specific
accessibility standards require separate design review.

N5: Business information is maintained across channels.
AI interpretation: Centralized content management.
Decision: Plausible inference. Automated syncing
is not supported by the source note.

N6: Promotional wording, images, and links need review.
AI interpretation: Sponsor content preview and approval.
Decision: Supported as a simulated preference.

N7: Visibility may be assessed using engagement measures.
AI interpretation: Analytics dashboard.
Decision: Metrics are suggested by the note, but a
dashboard is an unconfirmed implementation choice.

N8: Corrections and approved updates need coordination.
AI interpretation: Structured change request workflow.
Decision: Plausible inference. Revision history and
status notifications are not explicitly requested.

## Confirmation Gaps

1. Who has final authority to approve content?
2. Is external channel synchronization necessary?
3. Which engagement measures are useful?
4. Is responsive web access sufficient?
5. Who handles corrections, and within what timeframe?
6. Is simulated discovery acceptable to the instructor?

## Human Verification Decisions

Accepted:
- Five themes with source note references.
- Candidate requirements labeled hypothetical.
- Five initial uncertainties.

Qualified:
- Gemini described some preferences too confidently.
- An analytics dashboard is not directly requested.
- Automated synchronization is not supported.
- Revision logging and notification features need approval.

Rejected:
- Treating simulated notes as actual interview evidence.
- Presenting inferred features as confirmed requirements.
