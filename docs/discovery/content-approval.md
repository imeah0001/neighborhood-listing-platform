# Lab 4: Content Approval Workflow
Team E - Echo

Status: Proposed, not yet implemented.

## Applies To
- Property listings
- Local sponsor advertisements

## Workflow

Draft
  |
  v
Submitted for Review
  |
  v
Pending Admin Check
  |
  +-- Approved --> Approved and Published
  |
  +-- Rejected --> Rejected with Feedback
                       |
                       v
                     Draft

## Review Rules
1. Creator prepares and previews content.
2. Required fields must pass validation.
3. Content is submitted for review.
4. Reviewer checks details, images, and links.
5. Only approved content may be published.
6. Rejected content includes feedback.
7. Revised content must be reviewed again.
