# Lab 4: Simulated Discovery Notes

## Project
Neighborhood Listing Platform
Team E - Echo

## Evidence Status
SIMULATED PRACTICE MATERIAL - NOT A REAL INTERVIEW

These notes are hypothetical scenarios drafted with ChatGPT
to practice requirements discovery. No stakeholder was
interviewed. No actual responses or endorsements are claimed.

Instructor confirmation is still needed before treating
these scenarios as acceptable discovery evidence.

## Scenario A: Property Professional
Fictional role: Property manager

N1 - Workflow:
In this hypothetical scenario, the manager updates
property details in multiple places.

N2 - Content Approval:
The manager would prefer a review step before
a new or edited listing becomes public.

N3 - Success:
Accurate listings and fewer repeated corrections
might be useful measures of success.

N4 - Accessibility:
The manager may need listings that are easy to read
and navigate on both mobile and desktop devices.

## Scenario B: Local Sponsor
Fictional role: Neighborhood business owner

N5 - Workflow:
In this hypothetical scenario, the owner maintains
business information across several channels.

N6 - Content Approval:
The owner would want to check promotional wording,
images, and destination links before publication.

N7 - Success:
The owner might evaluate visibility using appropriate
engagement measures rather than private financial data.

N8 - Collaboration:
The owner may need a clear process for requesting
corrections and confirming approved updates.

## Important Limitations
- All eight notes are invented practice scenarios.
- None represents a verified stakeholder statement.
- No names, contact details, or sensitive data are stored.
- These are candidate needs, not confirmed requirements.
- Any product decision based on these notes requires
  stakeholder or instructor confirmation.
